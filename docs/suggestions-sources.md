# Suggestions — claims ledger

Method: one Wikipedia article per candidate, fetched 2026-10-21. Quotes are copied from the fetched page. A page reflects its last edit (most dated Aug–Oct 2026); "current" wording is used only when the page marks the post as current ("מכהן").

Base URL: `https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/` (abbreviated `he:`). `en:` = `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/`.

## Rules (revised 2026-10-21)

1. **Tiers**, applied to every party alike:
   - **A:** held the portfolio as full minister, or a top-level professional background in the field (Chief of Staff or Deputy Chief of Staff; head of the Shin Bet; Accountant General or head of the Finance Ministry's Budget Division; ambassador; IDF Spokesperson or head of the national hasbara body).
   - **B:** deputy minister, "minister in the ministry", director-general, chair of the matching Knesset committee or of its subcommittee for the field, or acting minister for under 6 months.
   - **C:** other professional background.
2. **Order within a tier:** currently serving first, then longer tenure.
3. **Party diversity:** at most one candidate per party in positions 1–4. Positions 5–6 repeat a party only when no other party has a qualifying candidate.
4. **pm:** slot-1 leaders of the parties, ordered by `parties[].seats`, top 6. The five parties tied at 8 seats keep their file order (joint, shas, otzma, utj, yb).
